How it started.

Founded amidst the rapid expansion of Hyderabad’s HITEC City and Gachibowli, Haven (Kgrand) began as a direct response to the fragmented and often unreliable PG landscape.

Our founders, former tech professionals themselves, experienced firsthand the disconnect between the high-pressure demands of the industry and the lack of high-quality, managed living spaces that offered more than just a bed.

They envisioned a "Haven"-a place where quality, professional governance, and human connection weren't luxury add-ons, but the standard for every resident.
