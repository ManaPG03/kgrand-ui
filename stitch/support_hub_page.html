Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about your stay with Kgrand.

Check-ins are available from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. Please ensure you have your digital ID and booking confirmation ready. Our on-site manager will guide you through the orientation and key handover.
We accept UPI, Bank Transfers, and Credit Cards through our secure payment links. Payments are due by the 5th of every month to avoid late fees.
Day guests are welcome until 10:00 PM. Overnight guests require prior approval from the property manager and may be subject to a nominal daily fee depending on the property type.
Currently, our Madhapur and Suites locations offer pet-friendly floors. Please check with the specific property manager for availability and pet registration guidelines.
